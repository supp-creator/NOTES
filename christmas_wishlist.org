* Fountain Pen Inks

Get me ink that comes in bottles or flasks, not those in small cartridges. I like the bottles. The cartridges may have proprietary designs, meaning they only fit in specific fountain pens from the same brand. But bottled ink can be used in any pen.

If the bottle advertises 'shading', that's good. I like ink that shifts colors.  Avoid inks that advertises 'shimmer', that's literally glitter. I normally don't like glittery inks. If it says 'sheen', that's okay. I don't really feel strongly about it.

For reference, ~2 mL is enough ink for a month so 30mL would definitely last a full year.

All of these inks can be bought online from their official websites or in Lazada & Shopee. You can find popular inks in large *Fully Booked* stores like in High Street or Mitsukoshi. Or in specialty stores like *Scribe* in SM Aura. You can also check *National Bookstore* but I'm pretty sure they don't have any of the premium inks.

** Specific Inks

This is just a reference guide to give you an idea of what to expect, to check if you're getting scammed, or if you're getting a bad price. I'm just putting information about the  inks you'll likely find here so you don't confuse them and can tell the differences.

*You can pick any color, I don't mind*. Just pick what you think looks nice; you could get plain black or something more flamboyant. Just don't get me inks that are all the same color .

The inks are listed in *descending order* of which I'd prefer.

*** Pilot Iroshizuku
#+attr_latex: :float nil :placement [H]
[[/home/tyrone/Downloads/inks/iroshizuku.jpg]]

Pilot's premium line of fountain pen ink. It has a core collection of 24 colors. I think they have the best looking bottles.They look like they're for perfume.

It comes in 50mL bottles, 15mL mini bottles, and 3mL cartridges. But the 15mL bottles don't really look very impressive. If you can, go for the 50mL bottle.

The ink itself is also very nice. I haven't seen any inks in an ugly color. They're also water-based so they write smoothly and don't clog fountain pens.

Estimated prices: ~1,100 PHP for the 50mL bottles. 

Suggested colors: *Tsuki-yo* or *Fuyu-syogun* or *Syo-ro* or *Sui-gyoku*. Like I said, they really don't have any bad colors.


*** Sailor Shikiori                                                                                                              
#+attr_latex: :float nil :placement [H]
[[/home/tyrone/Downloads/inks/shikiori.png]]

A premium, Japanese ink line themed around the four seasons. There's something like 32 different colors; that's 8 colors per season. You can choose any color for me.

This line of ink generally comes in 10mL and 20mL glass bottles. 50mL used to be sold in round bottles but have been discontinued since 2019 I think. I'd prefer anything above 10mL. I don't think just 10mL is worth it.

Suggested colors: *Shimoyo* or *Tamatebako* or *Fujisugata* or *Kasasagi*. But you can pick any according to availability. 


*** Sailor Ink Studio                                                                                                        
#+attr_latex: :float nil :placement [H]
[[/home/tyrone/Downloads/inks/ink_studio.png]]

Technically, Sailor's most premium ink line. It is pretty niche and experimental. There's around 50 colors but the number changes a lot since colors are constantly getting added and discontinued.

Unlike other Sailor inks, each color is designated by three digits.

The first digit scales from 0 to 9 with 0 being extremely light, pastel shades to 9 being deep, almost black tones.

The second digit indicates color family: 2 is for Gray/Black, 3 is for red/pink, 4 is for the Blues, 5 is for purples, 6 is for greens, 7s and 8s are for  yellows, oranges, and browns.

The third digit is for specific undertones.

So #123 is like a very light gray that has purple undertones.

They come only in 20mL glass bottles but those costs around ~1,000 PHP. Not very cost-effective. I'd only recommend purchasing them if you they're on sale.

I heard you can find standard colors for 675 in Scribe in SM Aura? But when I go, they're usually out of stock.

Go with colors *#123*, *#224*, or *#162*. In that order of preference. 


*** Lamy
#+attr_latex: :float nil :placement [H]
[[/home/tyrone/Downloads/inks/lamy.png]]

Lamy ink comes mainly in four different volumes: individual cartridges (labeled T10), plastic 30mL plastic bottles (T51), large 50mL glass bottles (T51), and high-end 30mL crystal bottles (T53).

The standard colors come in Blue (which is erasable with water), Black, Blue-Black, Red, Green, Turquoise, and Violet.

T51 bottles will likely cost ~500 PHP to ~600 PHP.

T52 bottles are sold officially on Lazada for 669 PHP. Prices are also 669 on their official Lamy website.

T53 bottles are sold I think for ~1,000 PHP.

The T52 bottles come in a few standard colors that are noted online to be muted and matte: *Turquoise* > *Petrol* > *Blue-Black* > *Dark Lilac* > *Black* > all the other colors.

While the T53 line have really saturated, intense dyed colors. The are themed after gemstones: *Amazonite* > *Agate* > *Obsidian* > *Benitoite* > all the others.

*** Platinum
#+attr_latex: :float nil :placement [H]
[[/home/tyrone/Downloads/inks/platinum.png]]

A top Japanese brand that offer different types of fountain pen inks.

There are _Standard Dye_ inks for everyday use. They come in cartridge packs that range from 130 PHP to 300 PHP while the 60mL bottles range from 750 PHP to 1,400 PHP depending on color and if they are limited editions.

There are _Pigmented Inks_. They are for archival uses and water-resistant. That means they're more prone to clogging pens but aren't affected by highlighters. They also come in cartridges or 60mL glass bottles. The glass bottles usually retail above 1,400 PHP.

There are the _Classic Inks (Iron-Gall)_. They are interesting because the inks oxidizes and darkens as they dry. The 60mL ink retail at 1,400 to around 1,500.

There are the _Mixable (Mix-Free)_ Inks that are special because they can be mixed with each other safely, without causing unintended chemical reactions or clogging to create new colors. They costs similarly to the higher-end Standard inks, so around 1,400 PHP per 60mL.

Lastly, there is the _Chou Kuro Black_. This is the most premium ink line Platinum offers. It is called the "blackest of the black" inks. It's double the price of Standard Ink: 2,800 PHP.

If you choose to get me a Platinum ink, avoid the Mixable line because their whole point is to be mixed with another color but that would need at least two bottles (2 x 1,400 = 2,800). That's way too expensive.

I'd like to try *Pigmented Ink Carbon Black* if you can find any. *Classic Sepia Black* too would be interesting.

*** Pilot Standard                                                                                                             
#+attr_latex: :float nil :placement [H]
[[/home/tyrone/Downloads/inks/pilot.png]]

I already have these. They come in three main colors: Blue, Black, Blue-Black. I think there's red.

There's another line of Pilot inks called *Pilot Namiki*, they are the same chemically with Pilot Standard. So don't fall for listings saying Namiki is a premium ink.

Pilot Standard isn't labeled Pilot Standard. They're just called Pilot [color] like Pilot Blue or Pilot Black.

A 30mL bottle comes in at ~200 PHP. It's what I normally buy. It is because of that, that I designate Pilot Standard as the baseline of fountain pen inks.

I prefer Blue-Black > Black > Blue >>> Red.
